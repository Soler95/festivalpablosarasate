The festival

A week of violin in Sarasate’s home town

The Pablo Sarasate International Violin Festival brings leading violinists and young performers to Pamplona for a week of concerts and masterclasses.

The second edition runs from 8 to 14 March 2027, the week of Sarasate’s birthday on 10 March. Concerts and classes take place at Pamplona Cathedral, the Pablo Sarasate Conservatory and Civivox.

Pablo Sarasate · 1844 – 1908

Pamplona’s violinist

Born in Pamplona, Pablo Sarasate was one of the great violin virtuosos of the nineteenth century. The composer of Zigeunerweisen and the Carmen Fantasy, he inspired Lalo, Bruch and Saint-Saëns to write some of the cornerstones of the violin repertoire for him.
